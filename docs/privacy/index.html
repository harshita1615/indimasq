Kavach Privacy Policy

Kavach browser extension · Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Kavach checks the text you paste or send, and the files you attach, on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini on your own device. It does not collect, store, sell or transmit your messages or any personal data. It makes no network requests at all.

What Kavach does

Kavach is a browser extension that warns you before you send Indian personal details, such as Aadhaar, PAN, UPI ID, IFSC, bank account, mobile number, GSTIN or email address, to an AI chatbot, and can replace them with placeholders like [PAN_1] before the message leaves your browser.

How your text is handled

  • When you paste into or send from the message box on chatgpt.com, claude.ai or gemini.google.com, Kavach reads that text and checks it for personal details using rules that run inside your browser.
  • The text is processed in memory only. It is never saved, logged, or sent to Kavach, the developer, or anyone else.
  • If you choose Mask, the details are replaced with placeholders in the message box. The link between a placeholder and the original value is kept in that browser tab's memory only and disappears when you close or reload the tab.
  • After you mask something, Kavach underlines its placeholders in the chat, such as [PAN_1] in a reply. When you point at one, Kavach shows the original value in its own small tooltip. The chat page keeps the placeholder: the value is not put into the page, and nothing is sent to the chatbot or anyone else. You can turn this off with Show real values on hover in the popup.
  • The popup's This chat list shows the placeholders and values of the tab you are looking at. It is read from that tab's memory when you open the popup and is never saved.
  • Kavach does not run on any other website and does not read your browsing history. It does not store or send the chat's contents.

How attached files are handled

  • When you attach a text, Word (.docx) or PDF file (with the file picker, by dragging it in, or by pasting it), Kavach reads its text on your device. PDFs are opened with PDF.js, which is bundled inside the extension. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is uploaded by Kavach.
  • The file is held back from the chat page until the check finishes (usually a second or two). If Kavach finds personal details, you choose: attach a safe copy, attach the original, or cancel.
  • In a safe copy, each value is replaced by a placeholder such as [PAN_1]. A Word or text file keeps its formatting. A PDF is redrawn as page pictures with each value covered by its placeholder, so none of the original text, metadata, form fields, comments or attachments is carried over.
  • Locked (password-protected), scanned or very long PDFs cannot be checked: Kavach says so, and you decide.
  • Files and their text are processed in memory only, inside the extension, and never stored. You can turn file checks off with Check attached files in the popup.

What is stored on your device

Kavach stores only the following, in your browser's local extension storage:

ItemExamplePurpose
On/off setting for each supported site ChatGPT: on Remembers where you want protection
Whether to check attached files and to show real values on hover On Remembers your choices from the popup
Count of items caught, by type PAN: 3 Shown to you in the extension's popup

These are numbers and on/off flags only. No text, values, website addresses or identifiers are stored. This data stays on your device and is not synced. You can clear the counts with Reset counts in the popup, and removing the extension deletes everything.

What Kavach does not do

  • No network requests, analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • No collection, sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No remote code: all of Kavach's code is included in the extension package.
  • No account or sign-in.

Permissions

  • Access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com: needed to check the text in those sites' message boxes before it is sent. Kavach has no access to other sites.
  • Storage: to save the on/off settings and counts described above, on your device.

Limitations

Kavach reduces the risk of sharing personal details, but it cannot guarantee that every detail is caught. Please check your messages before sending. Kavach is not legal advice and does not by itself make you or your organisation compliant with any law.
